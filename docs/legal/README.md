# Legal documents

`EULA.md`, `TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md` and `PRIVACY-POLICY.md` are shown in the app under Settings → Legal and on the download page.

Fill these fields before release: `[OWNER]` (your name or business entity), `[STATE]` (governing-law state), `[CONTACT EMAIL]`, `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`.

These were drafted by an AI assistant, not a lawyer. No document can guarantee freedom from claims. Have a licensed attorney review them before any public release, especially the limitation of liability, governing law and indemnity clauses, and the copyright-notice contact.
