# Cindrix Radio — Privacy Policy

Effective: [EFFECTIVE DATE] · Version 1.0 · Controller: [OWNER] · Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

## Summary
No account. No analytics, advertising or tracking. We run no server that receives your library, lyrics or listening history. Your data stays on your device.

## What stays on your device
Library index and file locations, playlists, favorites, listening history and progress, equalizer and app settings, recordings, downloads, accepted-terms version, and any lyrics you add. We never receive these. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data; the Backup & Restore tools only create files you choose to save.

## When data leaves your device
Only when you use a feature that needs the network, and then it goes to the service you chose, not to us:
- **Radio directory (Radio Browser):** searches and station lookups, and a "click" count when you play a station, as the directory's rules request. The directory operators can see your IP address and the request.
- **Stations and streams:** playing a stream connects you to the station's server, which can see your IP address and device information. Stations may collect data under their own policies.
- **Podcast feeds and downloads:** requests go to the feed and file hosts.
- **Station artwork and now-playing text:** fetched from the station or its site.
- **LAN sharing (off by default):** when you turn it on, only items you select are available to devices on your network. Lyrics you add are never shared.
- **Update checks:** only if you use the manual Check for Updates, which contacts the release host.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Permissions
Storage or drive access to read folders you choose and save to Downloads/Cindrix or a folder you select; network access for the features above; audio and notification/media controls for playback; microphone only if you use a feature that needs it, and only with your permission.

## Children
Not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Because the app collects no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete or share; if you believe otherwise, contact us.

## Your rights
Because we do not hold your data, access, correction and deletion are handled on your device through the app. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights; email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will respond.

## Security
Data is stored in your device's app storage. Use your device's own protections such as screen lock and encryption. No method is perfectly secure.

## Third parties
Third-party services you reach through the app have their own privacy practices that we do not control.

## Changes
We will update this policy as the app changes, show the new version in Settings → Legal, and ask you to accept material changes.
