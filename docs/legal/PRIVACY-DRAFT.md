# Cindrix Radio — Privacy Policy (DRAFT, not legal advice)

Last updated: 2026-11-08. Must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before release.

1. **No account, no tracking.** Cindrix Radio needs no account. It has no analytics, advertising or telemetry.
2. **Stays on your device.** Your library index, playlists, favorites, settings, recordings and any lyrics you add are stored only on your device. We do not receive or host them.
3. **When data leaves your device.** Only when you use a feature that needs it: radio directory searches and station playback, podcast feed downloads, and LAN sharing that you turn on. Those requests go to the services you choose (for example the Radio Browser directory or a station's own stream) and are subject to their policies.
4. **LAN sharing.** Off by default. When you turn it on, only the items you select are shared with devices on your network. Lyrics you added are never shared.
5. **Permissions.** Storage/drive access is used only to read the folders you choose and to save to your Downloads/Cindrix folder or the folder you select.
6. **Deleting data.** Removing the app or using Settings → Backup & Restore tools removes the local data. [Lawyer to confirm.]
